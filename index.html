Case 01ISAN3110
Modelling & Simulation
Grade 5 / 5

Factory cell production simulation

VisualComponents · Human-robot collaboration · University of Vaasa

I built a complete 3D factory cell simulating human-robot collaboration, then ran systematic bottleneck analysis against it. The inspection station was the constraint, so the fix was to parallelise inspection and rebalance the buffer ahead of it rather than speed up any single station. Throughput went from 55 to 130 units per hour across the iterations.

Case 03TUTA3240
Supply Chain Design
Grade 5 / 5

ABB Hurricane packaging optimisation

400 SKUs · 30+ box types · PPWR compliance

I analysed the packaging configuration across 400 SKUs and more than 30 box types, then developed two optimisation concepts. The chosen concept consolidates box types and tightens nesting, which cut material use by 12% and saved roughly 0.3 kg CO2e per shipping unit while staying fully compliant with PPWR.
